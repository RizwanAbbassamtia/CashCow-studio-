Edit room: mockup notes

A static mockup of the M5 timeline editor at 1440 × 900, drawn with HTML, CSS and SVG only (no images). Video: “Why she paid for strangers” (Kind Ledger, 12 scenes, 2:04), playhead at 1:05.6 in scene 7. Specification: EDITOR-DESIGN.md, section 4. Companion: Storyboard v2 mockup.

Legend

1
App rail

The rest of the app (Automation, Editing, Master Sheet, Settings), folded to icons so the editor gets the whole window.

2
Top bar

Which video, saved state, undo and redo, the Storyboard / Timeline switch, hand-off to other editors, the Render menu, checks and Ask Claude.

3
Library

Things you can add or apply: pictures, motion moves, transitions (hover a tile to see it play), popup and caption styles, music, and Ask Claude.

4
Monitor

Live preview drawn from the timeline as you edit. Green and red boxes are the start and end framing of the camera move; drag them.

5
Inspector

Every setting of what you selected, here scene 7. Closed sections show a one-line summary. Nothing selected shows the scene under the playhead.

6
Timeline toolbar

Select and blade tools, split, merge, delete, markers, popups, snapping, linked popups, lane size and zoom. Tooltips name the shortcut.

7
Overview strip

The whole video at a glance. The white bracket is the part shown below and the amber line is the playhead. Click to jump.

8
Ruler and loop

Time in minutes and seconds. The light band is the in-out range (scene 7) that loops while you work on it.

9
Markers

Ticks at every sentence start, the story section flag (The reason) and your own notes (blue diamond).

10
Popups and captions

Purple: short popups, linked to their scene (white outline). Brown: captions timed to the words; the current one is outlined.

11
Scenes

One clip per picture. Cuts move between sentences; the strip shows the camera move. Hatched bands are transitions, centred on the cut.

12
Voice

The narration, the clock of the video. Every word sits in the word bar; the word being spoken is white. Hatched blocks are pauses you can shorten.

13
Music

The track after ducking. The white line is its volume, dipping 12 dB under the voice and rising in the pauses.

14
Render menu

Pick sizes, see what is out of date, render, then approve and send to export. Shown open here; click Render to close it.
